# LUNAR menu image sources

Menu imagery in `assets/data/menu-items.json` uses direct Unsplash image URLs. Images are hotlinked, not bundled locally in this archive; the browser needs internet access to display them.

Source platform: https://unsplash.com/
License information: https://unsplash.com/license
Unsplash states that its images may be used for free under the Unsplash License, including commercial use, subject to the license terms. Attribution is not generally required but is appreciated. Review the terms before publishing, and avoid images with recognizable trademarks, artwork, or people unless you have the necessary rights.

Each item's `image_url` identifies the exact image asset currently configured. These links have not been individually HTTP-checked in this environment, so please spot-check them in the browser before publication.
